About TTN Finance

Last updated: October 18, 2026

TTN Finance is an English-language publication covering financial markets, technology and the global economy. We publish news explainers and analysis alongside financial calculators and a separately labeled news aggregator.

Our coverage

  • Market Analysis: markets, economic developments, companies, digital assets and financial regulation.
  • Tech & AI: artificial intelligence, software, semiconductors and the infrastructure behind technology.
  • News Aggregator: headlines and links to coverage published by other outlets. Those outlets are responsible for their own reporting.
  • Financial calculators: tools for exploring savings, loans, retirement and currency scenarios.

Editorial responsibility

Articles carrying the TTN Research Desk byline are published under a collective TTN Finance editorial identity. That byline does not identify an individual reporter or imply on-location reporting. TTN Finance is maintained through the GlobTechCo publishing project. Editorial questions and correction requests go to ttnfinance87@gmail.com.

How we prepare articles

We use company announcements, regulatory filings, official data and reporting from established publications. We distinguish reported facts from our analysis, attribute claims and forecasts, and identify important limits in the available evidence. A later news report does not change the date of the underlying event. Sources and relevant internal links are provided with articles.

AI tools assist with research, drafting, editing and illustrative images. Generated images are illustrations, not evidence of an event or photographs taken by TTN. They must not be read as proof of a transaction, arrest, location visit or product test.

Corrections and updates

To report an error, send the article URL, the disputed passage and supporting evidence through our Contact page. We assess requests and correct verified errors at the existing URL. Material corrections should explain what changed; publication and update dates should be distinguished.

Advertising and commercial relationships

TTN Finance is intended to be supported by advertising and may use affiliate links. Paid placements and active affiliate relationships should be disclosed where they appear. Commercial relationships do not determine our editorial conclusions. A broker listing is not a recommendation or confirmation of suitability.

Using market data and tools

Quotes may be delayed or unavailable. Calculators depend on the assumptions entered and do not predict returns. Our content is for general information, not personalized investment, financial, tax or legal advice.

Contact the publication

Contact TTN Finance for editorial feedback, corrections, privacy requests and business inquiries. See our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy for data-handling information.
