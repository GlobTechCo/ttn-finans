How this calculator works

Enter the loan principal, a fixed annual interest rate and the term in months. The result gives the monthly payment, total repayment and total interest for a fully amortizing loan.

The calculator assumes equal monthly payments and a constant nominal annual rate divided by 12. It excludes origination fees, insurance, taxes, balloon payments and lender-specific rounding. An APR that includes fees is not necessarily the same as the interest rate entered here.

The payment formula is P × r ÷ [1 − (1 + r)⁻ⁿ], where P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of payments. At 0% interest, payment = P ÷ n. A $12,000 loan over 12 months at 0% therefore costs $1,000 per month.

Common questions

Can I calculate a zero-interest loan?

Yes. Set the annual rate to zero. The monthly payment is the principal divided by the number of months.

Is this a mortgage payment calculator?

It calculates principal and interest on a fixed-rate amortizing loan. A full mortgage payment can also include taxes, insurance and other charges.
