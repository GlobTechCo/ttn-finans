Cookie Policy

Last updated:

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. TTN also uses browser local storage for preferences such as cookie consent.

2. How TTN uses cookies

We use essential storage to remember your cookie-consent choice. If analytics or advertising services are enabled, those services may use additional cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

3. Advertising and third-party services

TTN may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, in the future. Third-party providers may place cookies to measure advertising performance or personalize ads where permitted by law and your choices.

4. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect some site functionality. You can also change your browser's privacy settings at any time.

5. Changes

We may update this policy when the site's technology or advertising setup changes. The latest version will always be published on this page.

6. Contact

For questions about cookies or privacy, contact TTN through the information provided on the About page.
