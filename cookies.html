Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy describes cookies and browser storage used by TTN Finance. It applies to this publication; external services have their own policies. Read it with our Privacy Policy.

Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small values stored by a website in your browser. Local storage also remembers settings on your device, but is not sent with every page request. Our current site uses local storage for your appearance and analytics choices.

Analytics preferences

You can read the site without accepting analytics. Accept enables Google Analytics 4; Decline keeps it off. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in our analytics configuration.

The same control appears as Cookie preferences in every TTN footer. Changing to Decline disables further analytics collection through our integration. It does not erase data already sent to Google or automatically delete existing cookies. Use your browser's site-data controls to remove stored cookies and local storage. Clearing the consent setting causes the choice to appear again.

External services and advertising

Market-data requests, news images, fonts and charts can connect your browser to external providers independently of analytics consent. Declining TTN analytics is not a global opt-out from those providers. Their own policies govern any storage or request data they use; see Third-party services.

TTN's current pages do not load a Google AdSense advertising tag. The analytics preference control covers analytics only. If advertising tracking is introduced, this policy and the applicable consent controls must be updated before it is activated.
