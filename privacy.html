Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Who operates this website

This policy covers TTN Finance at globtechco.github.io/ttn-finans, maintained through the GlobTechCo publishing project. For privacy questions or requests, email ttnfinance87@gmail.com or use our Contact page.

Information involved in using TTN

You do not need an account to read TTN. Our pages do not ask you to register or submit financial account details. Your browser nevertheless sends technical request information, including an IP address, to the hosting service and any external providers whose resources it loads.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any information you include. We use that correspondence to answer your request, assess corrections or handle business inquiries. Please do not send passwords, banking credentials or sensitive documents.

Hosting and local tools

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. See GitHub Pages documentation and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Calculator arithmetic runs in your browser. We do not provide a server-side store for the personal amounts you enter. Currency and cryptocurrency converters request rates from data providers; those requests carry technical connection data. Our theme and analytics choices are saved locally as described in the Cookie Policy.

Optional Google Analytics

With your analytics acceptance, Google Analytics 4 measures page use, referrals and browser/device information. The Google tag is not loaded before acceptance. Decline keeps analytics off. Our integration disables Google signals and advertising personalization and does not send your name or email address to Analytics.

You may withdraw analytics acceptance through Cookie preferences in the footer. This stops further analytics collection through our integration; it does not remove information already collected or automatically delete existing cookies. See the Cookie Policy for storage names and browser controls and Google's partner-site privacy information.

Third-party services

Depending on the page and feature used, your browser may connect to CoinGecko and Frankfurter for prices and exchange rates, TradingView for charts, rss2json for aggregated news, Google Fonts for typography, and source websites or Pexels for news images. Additional quote or news integrations, such as Twelve Data or Finnhub, are used only when configured. Email is handled through Gmail.

These providers receive technical request information and apply their own policies. Relevant notices include CoinGecko, TradingView, rss2json, Pexels and Google. Analytics refusal does not prevent the separate requests needed to load these features or external images. Following an external article or broker link takes you to that provider's site.

Advertising and affiliate links

TTN is intended to be supported by advertising and may earn commissions from active affiliate links identified where they appear. The current site does not load a Google AdSense advertising tag. Our analytics choice is not an advertising-consent system. If advertising tracking is introduced, we will update these disclosures and provide the required choices before activating it.

Retention and international processing

Local preferences remain until changed or cleared. Optional Analytics cookies use Google's default duration of two years unless provider settings or browser limits shorten it. We cannot confirm the Analytics account's event-data retention setting from the website code. Correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and related editorial or business records; we do not currently publish a fixed deletion schedule.

Hosting, analytics and email providers may process information in countries other than yours. Their notices describe their processing, retention and international-transfer arrangements. We do not control their independent security logs.

Your choices and requests

You can decline or withdraw analytics, clear site data in your browser, and contact us about information you have sent us. Depending on your location, applicable law may give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal data, object to its use, withdraw consent or complain to a relevant supervisory authority. Email ttnfinance87@gmail.com with the nature of your request. We may need proportionate information to verify it. We can address records we control and explain when a request concerns an external provider.

Children and policy changes

TTN is a general-audience publication and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us. We will publish material changes to our practices here with a revised update date.
